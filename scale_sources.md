## SurveyBot3000 validation study — scale-generalization set

21 instruments, 260 items, 33,670 pairwise correlations. No item in this set occurs in the
training pool. Data: Hommel, B. E., & Arslan, R. C. (2025). Language models accurately infer
correlations between psychological items and scales from text alone.

`scale_name` is recovered from the SPSS variable label (`"<Scale name>: <item text>"`) by
`scale_from_label()` — variable-name prefixes are not unique in this file (`FRI_..` and `IFI_..`
